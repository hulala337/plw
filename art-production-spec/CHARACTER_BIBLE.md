# Pelican Workbench Character Bible

## Identity lock
- Species: pelican
- Silhouette: rounded, friendly, compact and soft
- Feathers: white to very light gray with restrained blue-gray shadows
- Bill: warm yellow/orange, rounded and clearly pelican-like
- Eyes: simple, expressive and stable in spacing and scale
- Head/body ratio: large rounded head, compact body
- Wings: expressive pelican wings, never humanoid arms
- Rendering: polished 2D/2.5D editorial illustration with soft light and subtle depth
- Personality: warm, intelligent, calm, occasionally playful

## State rules
neutral = relaxed and calm
working = attentive posture
Typing = natural wing/keyboard interaction
thinking = visible pause and thoughtful posture
focused = forward posture and concentrated eyes
tired = slightly lowered posture
resting = relaxed away from work
sleeping = peaceful closed eyes
drinking_coffee = coherent cup interaction
celebrating = restrained celebration
greeting = welcoming gesture
encouraging = supportive gesture
mini = same identity at small scale

## Hard rejection conditions
- bill shape/color materially changes
- eye spacing materially changes
- head/body ratio materially changes
- feather silhouette becomes a different character
- wings become human hands/arms
- extra limbs or duplicated body parts
- photorealistic face
- plastic 3D treatment
- generic unrelated bird
